## Interpretation

Generator capacity is not energy output. G=C enables self-supply in constrained hours and can remain idle when grid electricity is cheaper. S1 demand remains in S2; weeks reset. Different buses can share the system generation ceiling and overlapping transmission constraints. Neither site count nor a Part 1 signature alone proves independent capacity or economic superiority. Selected hourly tests keep admitted loads/resources fixed and do not change the campaign score or prove a full-horizon allocation gain.
