### What drove the score differences

**The 200 MW generator at bus 6141** cleared 499.9 MW in all 36 fields and reached 500 MW in 35. In its lowest-award field, nine rivals each used 50 MW generation and 125 MW / 500 MWh storage at bus 6141. The evaluator awarded 499.943942 MW in both S1 rounds. Line b02136 was at its lower flow limit during the seven modeled hours when our generator ran. [200 MW limiting field](<Battery_S1_G200_P0__nine_g50_b125_at_6141_campaign.md>)

At the same S1 bus, the **97/99 hybrid** saved $67.4M per S1 build and earned $144.3M more on average. The hybrid’s worst-case score was $163.5M higher. The 200 MW build provided 42.4 kW more minimum S1 allocation. Across both graded weeks, the hybrid gained $137.3M in S1 and $7.0M in S2 on average.

**Garcia’s 225 MW generator at bus 2054** cost $50.0M more per S1 build than the 200 MW generator at bus 6141. Garcia earned $332.2M more on average and $532.9M more in the worst field. The two generator-only builds differ in S1 bus and generator size.

Compared with 97/99, Garcia earned $224.4M less in S1 and $412.3M more in S2 on average. All 6 plans received 500 MW in every S2 round. Garcia’s S1 carryover was followed by $622.8M less S2 generator operating cost and $210.5M more S2 grid energy cost. The net mean campaign advantage was $187.9M.

Carter’s battery earned the highest campaign score in 7 fields. In its weakest field, nine rivals each used a 196 MW / 784 MWh battery at bus 6141. The evaluator awarded Carter 0.004263 MW and each rival about 495.263 MW in S1, for a field total of about 4,457 MW. [Carter’s limiting field](<Battery_S1_G0_P125__nine_b196_at_6141_campaign.md>)

## Methods

Each field uses the same ten 500 MW requests, with the nine rivals following the saved field definition. The 36 fields include the 21 existing cases and 16 same-bus adversarial combinations with one overlap. The course evaluator dispatches 120 periods with four-hour storage and daily state-of-charge cycles. The graded rounds are W2S1, W2S2, W3S1, and W3S2. Each week resets, then the net hourly S1 withdrawals of all ten teams enter that week’s S2 base load. Each plan requests a new 500 MW site with 500 MW of generation at bus 6141 in S2. The 200 MW generator-only results reuse the official campaigns from the targeted sizing study. The 210 MW build ran through the same four rounds for every field. The report rechecks artifact hashes, matching rival submissions, round scores, and each weekly carryover. Campaign scores sum the four rounds. The exact and near-firm field counts require the corresponding S1 award in both graded weeks. The capital column shows the requested S1 build cost.
