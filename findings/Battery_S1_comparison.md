The native grid falls below the full 5,000 MW field request in hours **106, 107, 108, 109, 110, 111, 112**, representing 7 hours per year. A four-hour battery costs $1.4M per MW and a generator costs $2M per MW. Every day starts and ends at 50% SOC. The battery shifts energy from hours with headroom and its grid charging also becomes part of S1 carryover into S2.

With nine 200 MW generators at bus 6141, serving the entire field at that bus would still require **195.505 MW of our own backup** at the tightest hour. Each rival can cut its grid draw to 300 MW and the standalone line-limited grid envelope is 3,004.495 MW. The joint allocation can also reduce rival awards, which is why this fixed-full-field bound is paired with observed admission.
